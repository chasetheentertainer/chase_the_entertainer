What You're Actually Getting

Can I send it straight to the person I'm gifting?

Yes. After checkout, email Chase@chasetheentertainer.com with their name and a short message, and the voucher can go to you or straight to them.

How long does it last?

About as long as a good cup of coffee, usually 30 to 45 minutes. Nobody's holding a stopwatch.

Where?

Any café you like around Colorado Springs. Somewhere farther? Reach out and we'll talk.

Does the voucher expire?

No. Book whenever a date works.

Something came up?

It happens. Just let Chase know and pick a new date.

Is this a show?

Sort of. It's coffee with a guy who happens to do impossible things. Relaxed, personal, and happening right at your table. The people at the next table will be jealous. That's not on you.

Will Malini be there?

Malini does not drink coffee. He has opinions about it, though.
